Team reflection

This one happens away from the screen. Print the worksheet, gather your team, and talk through how your everyday decisions affect the wider supply chain.

Take the conversation into your team

Discuss with your colleagues

20–30 minutes · No scoring · Discuss away from the screen

1

Think beyond your own objectives

What is one decision our team regularly makes that improves our own results but may create extra work, cost, or risk elsewhere in the company?

2

Involve other functions early

Which function should we involve earlier in our decision-making, and what would we ask them?

3

Make trade-offs explicit

What is one trade-off our team rarely discusses openly?

Examples to spark discussion

Cost vs ServiceInventory vs AvailabilitySpeed vs SustainabilityEfficiency vs Resilience

Bonus questions

When did we last make a decision that was good for us but perhaps not so good for the supply chain?
